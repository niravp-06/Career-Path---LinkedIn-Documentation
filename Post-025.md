## Milestone
**Last academic day as a student — final classroom experience.**
Last time sitting with classmates, last conversations about
assignments, college criteria, faculties, and exams.

---

## The Reality of the Shift

Conversations that were once general — assignments, exams,
college life — will from now on become purely individual:

- What specific project are they working on
- What report have they made for themselves
- Which internship or job they are pursuing

> *"This is the biggest hit that I got when I sat down
> for a while."*

---

## Honest Reflection

Student life has practically ended — not officially yet,
but in every real sense.

**What the remaining college time becomes:**
A preparation window — for the internship/job search
or higher studies planning.

No more lectures. No more reason to come to college
and spend time with classmates and friends in that way.

**What replaces it:**
> *"Now there is only personal time — to sit and work
> on goals and make them possible, that I always wished
> for from childhood."*
